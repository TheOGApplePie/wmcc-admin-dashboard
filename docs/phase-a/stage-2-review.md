## User-approved choices and remaining questions

**Already chosen:** Existing announcements remain published as grandfathered content; saving a material edit withdraws the announcement until approval. Email is last. Do not reopen those choices. The current social 047 implementation holds overdue scheduled posts; the safe recovery of failed posts remains unresolved and requires correction.

1. **Self-approval:** May someone with both edit and publish approve their own announcement or email? Recommendation: allow it initially, with the approving identity recorded, unless the organization requires a second reviewer. Restricting it improves separation of duties but may leave a small team unable to publish; it requires retaining submitter identity.
2. **Unknown social delivery:** When the provider cannot prove whether a post was sent, should staff confirm the outcome manually before any retry? Recommendation: yes. This avoids duplicate posts but may delay communication; some provider APIs cannot conclusively reconcile every request.
3. **Registration source and closure:** Who owns the Cognito submission integration, and can we inspect a redacted payload and its ingestion/closure configuration? Recommendation: verify these before building timestamp filtering or claiming a hard close. Hiding the public embed is possible sooner but does not stop direct form submissions.
4. **Email staging:** Should the last Phase A increment provide CMS drafting, templates and approval only, with no MailerLite account connection or sending? Recommendation: yes, while testing Maildeno as a candidate rather than committing to it. A separate decision is needed for provider, plan, consent/audience source and late registrations. What is the URL for the intended Open Email project?
5. **Retention and access:** Who may see registrant contact data and announcement review history, and should deletion retain historical content? Recommendation: keep existing `events.view` access until a narrower role is explicitly defined; retain announcement revision history subject to a documented retention/deletion policy. This preserves continuity but does not establish that current access is appropriate.

## Review of proposed sequence

1. Correct direct announcement approval revision enforcement and overdue failed-post recovery; retain user choices and review existing uncommitted changes rather than discarding them.
2. Test announcement public and staff-cookie reads, browser states, version conflicts, RLS/grants and deployment/cache behavior against the full migration chain. Decide and implement event association only within the independent module; never add event lifecycle triggers here.
3. Verify Cognito source/provenance and implement registration state/visibility only with a clearly described enforcement boundary. Window-based selection remains unavailable when timestamps cannot be trusted.
4. Harden social uncertain-outcome reconciliation and provider-specific retry without duplicating the scheduler. Confirm actual GitHub/Lightsail deployment and 4:5 generator ownership; preserve Story publishing.
5. After decisions above, implement provider-independent email staging last. Provider sync/activation/sends require separate approval and account-specific feasibility.
